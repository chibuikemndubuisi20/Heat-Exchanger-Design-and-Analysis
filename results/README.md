# Results

This folder contains the main plots produced from the heat exchanger temperature data using Python.

## Plots

- **Temperature vs Time** – shows the hot-water bath cooling, the cold-water inlet remaining stable and the collection vessel warming during the experiment.

- **Apparent Heat-Transfer Rate vs Time** – shows how the calculated heat-transfer rate changed during the experiment.

- **LMTD vs Time** – shows how the temperature driving force for heat transfer changed as the hot bath cooled.

- **Apparent Overall Heat-Transfer Coefficient vs Time** – shows the calculated overall heat-transfer performance of the heat exchanger.

The heat-transfer rate and overall heat-transfer coefficient are described as apparent values because the cold-side outlet temperature was measured in the collection vessel rather than directly at the copper-tube outlet.
